AI governance, enforced

Govern every AI call
your organisation makes.

Author policy once, enforce it inline on your AI traffic, and turn every decision into audit-ready compliance evidence, across the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, SOC 2, NIST and more.

Author policy in the UI

Build rules from a visual match builder, tag each to the regulatory controls it satisfies, and test it before you ship. No YAML.

Enforce at the edge

A proxy on stock Envoy + OPA intercepts only the AI endpoints you register and applies your policy per request: allow, block, or step up.

Prove compliance

Every decision flows back into a per-control coverage matrix and signed, audit-ready conformance reports.

Author a rule→ Compile to policy→ Enforce on AI traffic→ Report & evidence

Built on stock Envoy and Open Policy Agent. We own the policy and the compliance mapping, not the data path.
