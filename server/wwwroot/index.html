Author policy once, enforce it inline on your AI traffic, and turn every decision into audit-ready compliance evidence, across the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, SOC 2, NIST and more.

Author policy in the UI

Build rules from a visual match builder, tag each to the regulatory controls it satisfies, and test it before you ship. No YAML.

Enforce at the edge

Prove compliance
